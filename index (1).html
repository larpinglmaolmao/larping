How to read this

BBWP is the percentile rank of current Bollinger Band width against recent history. It is a volatility reading, not a direction.

COMPRESSED — coiled, not moving. A watchlist state, not an entry.
COILING — heading into the freeze.
FIRING / WAKING — the squeeze is releasing. Direction is not implied: expansion resolves down as often as up.
HOT — volatility already expanded. Late.

Dots show 1D, 3D and 1W. All three blue is multi-timeframe compression, the highest-conviction version. Range is high-to-low across the compression window and gives you a defined invalidation level.

Setup quality is a heuristic ranking, not a backtested edge. Nothing here is investment advice.
